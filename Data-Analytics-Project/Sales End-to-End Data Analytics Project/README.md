# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard – Power BI

> **An interactive Business Intelligence dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and customer insights. This project demonstrates Business Analyst and Data Analyst skills through KPI tracking, data visualization, and actionable business recommendations.**

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales Performance Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of business performance using interactive visualizations and KPIs. It enables decision-makers to monitor sales trends, analyze regional performance, evaluate profitability, and identify growth opportunities.

Designed with a clean corporate theme, the dashboard focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance.
* Track key business KPIs.
* Monitor regional profitability.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Understand customer contribution.
* Support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query**
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**
* **CSV Dataset**

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains transactional sales information including:

* Order ID
* Order Date
* Customer
* Product
* Region
* Sales
* Cost
* Profit

---

# 📈 Dashboard Features

### 📌 Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance through interactive KPI cards and charts.

**KPIs**

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Average Order Value
* Profit Margin
* Total Orders

**Visualizations**

* Monthly Sales Trend
* Regional Profit Analysis
* Sales by Product
* Interactive Filters
* Executive Business Insight

---

## 🎛 Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the report by:

* Year
* Region
* Product
* Customer

All visuals update automatically based on the selected filters.

# 📊 Business Insights

The dashboard highlights key findings such as:

* Healthy overall profit margin indicating efficient cost management.
* Balanced regional sales distribution reducing geographical dependency.
* Product-wise sales comparison for inventory and marketing decisions.
* Monthly sales trends to identify seasonal patterns.
* Regional profitability analysis for strategic planning.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

* Increase marketing efforts for high-profit products.
* Improve sales strategies in lower-performing regions.
* Focus on customer retention programs.
* Increase average order value through cross-selling and upselling.
* Monitor monthly sales fluctuations to improve forecasting accuracy.
